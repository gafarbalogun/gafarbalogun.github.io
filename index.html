Incidents I'm proud of

Jenkins pipeline — cascading failure

A blocked PowerShell.exe alert cleared from a security standpoint, but the Jenkins pipeline it touched kept failing. No one from cyber was looped into the incident that got raised. Stepped in as the bridge — traced it back through prior alerts, found it was a recurring threshold issue, and re-applied a prior fix to extend the timeout. Resolved.

/etc/passwd detection — pod replication gap

A detection tuned to catch modifications to sensitive files like /etc/passwd didn't account for pod replication — a new pod writing fresh credentials on startup looks identical to tampering. Catching it took direct knowledge of how Kubernetes scheduling and replication actually behave. Fix: in progress.
